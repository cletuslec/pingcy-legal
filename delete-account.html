Delete your PingCy account

In the app (immediate)

Open PingCy, go to Account, and choose Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your profile, preferences, offer activity, and login are erased, and reviews you wrote are removed.

If you can no longer access the app

Email support.mokapp@gmail.com from the email address of your PingCy account with the subject "Delete my PingCy account". The account and its associated data will be deleted, and you will receive a confirmation reply.

What is deleted

Your profile, area and preference settings, offer activity history, reviews you wrote, and your login. A venue's rating of you is detached from your identity. No data is retained after deletion except records we are legally required to keep, if any.
