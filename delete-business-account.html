Delete your PingCy Business account

This page is for the PingCy Business: Venue Offers app (package com.pingcy.business), published on Google Play and the App Store by PingCy, the developer of the PingCy apps. Members of the consumer app should use Delete your PingCy account instead.

Request deletion from the app

Open PingCy Business, go to Account, and choose Delete account. The app prepares an email to support@pingcy.com from your sign-in address with the subject "Delete my business account". Because live offers and issued codes have to be unwound safely, the deletion runs through support and is completed within 30 days, with a confirmation reply when done.

If you can no longer access the app

Email support@pingcy.com from the email address of your PingCy Business account with the subject "Delete my business account". We verify the address and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your business account and login, your venues and their details, every offer you published, redemption history and issued codes, guest ratings you gave, and your profile. Members' own accounts are not affected. No data is retained after deletion except records we are legally required to keep, if any.
