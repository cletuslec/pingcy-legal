PingCy Privacy Policy

Draft, last updated 14 October 2026. PingCy is a working name; the operating company will be named here once formed.

What we collect

Your email address and password (held by our authentication provider), the neighbourhood area you choose, your search radius, your category interests, your quiet hours, your notification preference, a timestamp of when the app was last open, the days on which you opened the app, the places you asked for offers from, and your offer activity: invitations sent to you, your accepts and passes, redemption codes, and reviews you write.

Location is optional. If you allow it, the app reads your phone's position while you use it to show how far each place is. That position stays on your phone: the last one is kept there, rounded to about 100 m, for when location is off, and it is never sent to us. If you choose Use my current location, we store one point rounded to about 500 m as your home anchor. If you turn on Only offers I can reach, the app sends one point rounded to about 100 m while it is open, and each new point overwrites the last. We never keep a location history.

Why we collect it

To run the service you signed up for: matching nearby offers to your stated preferences, ordering invitations fairly, issuing redemption codes, and showing venues a rating for guests they host. Fairness ordering uses only your own activity: the chances you had this month and your member level, which counts the days you opened the app, the invitations you answered, your visits and the places you asked for, over the last 30 days. Days you opened the app are deleted after 60 days. We do not profile you for advertising, we show no third-party ads, and we sell nothing about you to anyone.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt). Access is restricted by row-level security: venues never see other venues' data, and other customers never see yours beyond the first name and rating attached to reviews you choose to publish.

Photos, ratings, opening hours and reviews credited to Google Maps are loaded by your phone from Google's servers, which, like any website, see your device's internet address. We send Google nothing about you. Google's privacy policy covers that content (policies.google.com/privacy).

How long, and how to erase it

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account yourself in Account, which permanently erases your profile, activity, and login. Reviews you wrote are removed; a venue's rating of you is detached from your identity.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, and portability of your data, and you can complain to the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (dataprotection.gov.cy). To exercise them, write to support@pingcy.com.
