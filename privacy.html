PingCy Privacy Policy

Draft, last updated 11 August 2026. PingCy is a working name; the operating company will be named here once formed.

What we collect

Your email address and password (held by our authentication provider), the neighbourhood area you choose, your search radius, your category interests, your quiet hours, your notification preference, a timestamp of when the app was last open, and your offer activity: invitations sent to you, your accepts and passes, redemption codes, and reviews you write. We never collect or store your GPS position or any location history. Your area is a neighbourhood you pick by hand.

Why we collect it

To run the service you signed up for: matching nearby offers to your stated preferences, ordering invitations fairly, issuing redemption codes, and showing venues a rating for guests they host. Fairness ordering uses only your own activity counts. We do not profile you for advertising, we show no third-party ads, and we sell nothing about you to anyone.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt). Access is restricted by row-level security: venues never see other venues' data, and other customers never see yours beyond the first name and rating attached to reviews you choose to publish.

How long, and how to erase it

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account yourself in Account, which permanently erases your profile, activity, and login. Reviews you wrote are removed; a venue's rating of you is detached from your identity.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, and portability of your data, and you can complain to the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (dataprotection.gov.cy). A dedicated support contact will be published here before launch.
